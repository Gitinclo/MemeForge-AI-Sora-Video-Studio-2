# Style Guide: Chapter 1 Pilot ("Nocturne in the Rainy Bookstore")

## 1. Visual Style (`style_id: gothic_rain_anime_2026`)
- **Aesthetic**: Stylized dark-fantasy 2.5D anime cinema with atmospheric rain simulation, chiastic contrast between cold slate-blue storm light outside and warm tungsten desk-lamp glow inside, subtle 35mm film grain.
- **Aspect Ratio & Framing**: 16:9 widescreen (`1080p` pilot master, `720p` animatic/bake-off).
- **Color Palette**: Stormy slate shadows (`#0B1120`), warm amber tea/lamp glow (`#F59E0B`), crimson ember accents (`#E11D48`), and pale porcelain highlights (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Motion Rules**: 24fps animation cadence; measured, deliberate framing inside the shop contrasted with sharp kinetic cuts in the rain-soaked exterior.

## 2. Characters (3 Approved Visual Turnarounds)

- **Visual Anchor**: Young man with pale complexion, slightly tousled dark hair, tailored all-black shirt and trousers, calm welcoming expression with warm amber lamp reflections in dark eyes.
- **Voice Profile**: `voice_proprietor_baritone_calm` (warm, unhurried, hospitable; target `-16 LUFS` dialogue stem).

## 3. Locations (2 Approved Environments)

### `loc_rainy_bookstore` — The Cramped Old Bookstore Interior
- **Visual Anchor**: Narrow, towering floor-to-ceiling wooden bookshelves, stacks of volumes partially blocking the second-floor stairs, dust-frosted transom window, and a single warm yellow counter lamp illuminating two steaming teacups.
- **Ambient Audio Stem**: `sfx_bookstore_rain_on_glass_and_clock.wav` (`-28 LUFS` bed).

- **Ambient Audio Stem**: `sfx_heavy_downpour_and_distant_thunder.wav` (`-24 LUFS` bed).
