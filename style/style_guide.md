# Style Guide: Chapter 1 Pilot ("The Clockwork Horizon")

## 1. Visual Style (`style_id: cel_cinema_2026`)
- **Aesthetic**: Stylized hand-painted 2.5D cel-shaded animation with anamorphic depth of field, warm tungsten rim lighting, and subtle film grain.
- **Aspect Ratio & Framing**: 16:9 widescreen (`1080p` pilot master, `720p` animatic/bake-off).
- **Color Palette**: Deep Prussian blue shadows (`#0F172A`), brass amber highlights (`#F59E0B`), and oxidized copper midtones (`#14B8A6`).
- **Motion Rules**: 24fps cinematic motion cadence; avoid erratic handheld shake unless specified in action beats.
